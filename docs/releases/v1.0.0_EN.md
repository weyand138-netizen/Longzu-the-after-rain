*After the Rain* is a free, non-commercial, unofficial *Dragon Raja* fan visual novel. From Lu Mingfei's limited point of view, accompany Erii through seven days and make choices through observation, questions, preparation, and responsibility.

## About the Game

The story begins on a rain-soaked station platform. Over seven days, brief moments of ordinary life, questions and responses, and the truth and possible paths ahead form a shared journey.

Pay attention to what Erii writes, the objects she hands over, and her reactions in moments of silence. Decide when to explain, when to wait, and how to prepare for what comes next. Earlier choices echo through later scenes, shaping mutual understanding and the eventual outcome.

The game progresses through reading, scene presentation, and branching choices. It includes six deterministic endings, each with its own epilogue. Outcomes follow accumulated choices rather than a random selection.

## Content and Reading Features

- A prologue and seven days of story, with six endings and their epilogues.
- 18 ending event CGs, chapter scenery, character actions, and prop presentation.
- Background music, ambience, and story sound effects.
- Save/load, rollback, skip, automatic reading, and font settings.

The game is in Simplified Chinese and currently has no recorded character voice acting. This English information page does not indicate an English translation of the game.
